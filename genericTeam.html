Current Leadership

Dr. Stephanie Kamunya possesses a unique combination of scientific expertise, malaria-specific research experience, quantitative leadership skills, and translational research capabilities that position her exceptionally well to lead a hub focused on both malaria modelling and drug discovery.

Her academic and professional career has been dedicated to understanding and combating malaria from multiple perspectives, ranging from the molecular biology of the parasite to population-level disease dynamics. She earned a PhD in Computational Chemistry from the University of Cape Town, where her doctoral research focused on the design of selective Plasmodium falciparum kinase inhibitors using computer-aided drug discovery approaches. This work provided firsthand experience in target identification, medicinal chemistry, computational drug design, and the early stages of antimalarial drug development.

In addition to her drug discovery expertise, Dr. Kamunya has built a strong track record in malaria epidemiology and disease modelling. As a Senior Research Officer at the Centre for Malaria Elimination, Mount Kenya University, she leads malaria transmission modelling analyses that generate policy-relevant insights for malaria control and elimination strategies. She develops advanced analytical workflows and translates complex modelling outputs into actionable recommendations for public health stakeholders.

Her postdoctoral work at the Centre for Epidemiological Modelling and Analysis (CEMA) and the Modelling and Simulation Hub, Africa (MASHA) further strengthened her expertise in infectious disease modelling, intervention assessment, and public health strategy. Through these roles, she has collaborated with multidisciplinary teams to analyze disease trends, evaluate intervention effectiveness, and support evidence-based decision-making.

Importantly, Dr. Kamunya's career bridges two critical pillars of a modern malaria company: innovation in antimalarial products and evidence-driven deployment strategies. Her understanding of drug discovery enables her to guide research teams through the scientific and strategic challenges of developing new malaria therapeutics. At the same time, her expertise in malaria transmission modelling allows her to assess where, when, and how new products can have the greatest impact, ensuring that research investments are aligned with real-world disease burden and market needs.

Her publication record further demonstrates this breadth of expertise. She has contributed to research advancing the medicinal chemistry progression of compounds for malaria treatment while also conducting extensive malaria modelling research that integrates epidemiology, public health policy, and computational methods.

Beyond her technical credentials, Dr. Kamunya has demonstrated leadership through research supervision, scientific training, stakeholder engagement, and the facilitation of malaria modelling workshops. She has successfully communicated complex scientific concepts to diverse audiences, built collaborative relationships across institutions, and supported capacity building in malaria research. These are essential skills for leading multidisciplinary teams comprising drug discovery scientists, epidemiologists, data scientists, clinicians, and public health experts.

Dr. Kamunya would bring the rare ability to oversee the entire innovation pipeline, from identifying and optimizing novel antimalarial candidates to evaluating their impact through advanced disease modelling and informing deployment strategies. Her combination of drug discovery expertise, malaria modelling leadership, scientific credibility, and public health insight uniquely positions her to lead this organization working to develop and deliver transformative solutions for malaria control and elimination.
