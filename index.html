About the Hub

Drug Discovery Hub Kenya is a collaborative research and innovation platform dedicated to accelerating the discovery and development of safe, effective, and affordable medicines for diseases that disproportionately affect African populations.

By bringing together scientists, industry partners, we aim to transform cutting-edge research into health solutions that improve lives across Kenya and beyond.
